Your knitting and crochet notebook

Purlnote is an iPhone app for following patterns and keeping your notes in one place.

  • Read your pattern PDFs with highlights, pen and pinned notes that stay where you left them.
  • Keep your place with a row ruler and row and repeat counters.
  • Follow a pattern one step at a time, with just your size's numbers.
  • Keep projects, notes and photos together, on your device or synced with your Ravelry account.

Everything works offline, and nothing leaves your device unless you connect Ravelry.
