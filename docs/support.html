Common questions

Do I need a Ravelry account?
No. Choose “Start on this phone” and everything works on your phone. You can connect Ravelry later from More, or never.

How do I add a pattern?
In Library, import a PDF from Files. With Ravelry connected, the patterns you bought on Ravelry are listed there too, ready to download.

How do I move the row ruler?
Use the up and down arrows to move it a little at a time, or press and hold the ruler and drag it to another row.

The steps show the wrong size's numbers.
Tap the size button at the top of Steps to choose another size. If a step is still wrong, press and hold it to fix it by hand, and please email me the pattern's name so I can improve how sizes are read.

How do I see the tour again?
More → How Purlnote works.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?
PDFs, notes, highlights and counters are kept only on your phone, so deleting the app deletes them. With Ravelry connected and notes backup on, a copy of your notes and counters stays in your Ravelry account. See the privacy policy.
